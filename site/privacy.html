MAPMAN PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated 6 October 2026

MapMan is a tilt-to-move maze game made by one developer in Ireland. It has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. This page says what the game keeps, what it can send, and how to change your mind.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

Your progress, scores, settings, saved runs, wardrobe and drafting table levels are stored only on your phone. Level codes leave it only when you share one yourself.

The camera is used only while the scan sheet is open, to read a level's QR code. No picture is kept or sent anywhere.

PLAY STATS (ONLY IF YOU SAY YES)

The game asks once whether it may send anonymous play stats. It sends nothing unless you choose YES, SEND PLAY STATS, and you can turn them off any time in Options > PRIVACY.

When they are on, the game sends events such as a level being started, cleared, lost (with the tile where it happened and the time left) or quit, the tutorial being finished, and the steering and language settings in use. Each event carries a random ID made on your phone when you said yes. It is not linked to your name, account, advertising ID or device ID.

Turning play stats off stops them at once, deletes the random ID from your phone and drops any events not yet sent. Turning them on again starts a new, unrelated ID.

The stats go to PostHog, which stores them in the EU (Frankfurt) as our processor, and are kept for up to one year. They are used only to see which levels are too hard or too easy and to make the game better. They are never sold or used for advertising.

The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before turning play stats on.

ADS

Versions of MapMan with ads use Google AdMob. In the EU, the UK and other places where the law requires it, Google's own consent form asks before any personalised ads are shown, and Options lets you change that choice. Google's use of data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask to see or delete the play stats sent from your phone. Options > PRIVACY shows the first characters of your stats ID while play stats are on: quote it in your request so it can be found. Without that ID the stats can't be tied to you.

To ask, or with any question about this policy, open an issue at github.com/danbhala/MapMan/issues.

You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland, or to the data protection authority where you live.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
